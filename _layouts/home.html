University of Sheffield
Research Software Engineering

Through comprehensive programmes and collaborations, the RSE team's objective is to lead, support and guide the research community in leveraging software and data engineering to produce excellent research.

As data and research methods are becoming more complex and more computationally demanding, our mission is to ensure that all researchers and aspiring researchers have access to the necessary expertise to be proficient in developing and maintaining advanced research software tools.

The RSE team is supported by the School of Computer Science, IT Services and the School of Mathematical and Physical Sciences.

Collaborate

The RSE team collaborates on more than 30 projects a year. Collaborations span various lengths, from a few days or a few weeks to longer term partnerships of multiple years.

Learning

The RSE Team participates in a wide range of training and teaching activities. We provide training for people who work with code for research to build their skills in areas that academic training might not provide.

We have developed training courses for the University of Sheffield which have been recognised and adopted by external organisations. Additionally, we lead and co-lead academic modules at undergraduate and PhD levels within the university.
